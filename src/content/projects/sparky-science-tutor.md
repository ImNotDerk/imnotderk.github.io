## Overview

**SPARK+Y** is an educational AI chatbot designed to introduce elementary science concepts to Grade 3 students through interactive, story-driven explanations rather than static textbook answers.

## Contributions & Architecture

- **Frontend Interface**: Contributed to the conversational web UI using **React (Next.js)** and **Tailwind CSS**, keeping interactions approachable and focused for young learners.
- **Pedagogical AI Backend**: Built and integrated the **Python (FastAPI)** backend communicating with **Google Vertex AI / Gemini** endpoints to structure concept explanations into age-appropriate narratives.
