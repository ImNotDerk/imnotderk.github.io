## Overview

Built in a 48-hour sprint for **Developer Camp Manila 2026**, **BiyaHey!** finished as a **Top 16 Finalist out of 53 teams** by tackling information fragmentation across Philippine public transport networks.

## Highlights & Architecture

- **48-Hour Hackathon MVP**: Spearheaded ideation and built a high-fidelity frontend prototype using **React** and **TypeScript** to unify disparate route data into a single intuitive interface.
- **Decoupled Backend Auth (`api/`, `login/`)**: Paired the SPA with **Django 6**, **Django REST Framework**, `django-allauth`, `dj-rest-auth`, and `djangorestframework-simplejwt` (`GoogleOAuth2Adapter`) to exchange Google Sign-In tokens for stateless JWT access and refresh credentials.
